📋 Exam Instructions

Please read everything carefully before you start. Parents/teachers can help the child read.

1. About the exam

  • This is a computer-based multiple-choice (MCQ) exam: IPA Maths Olympiad – Grade 3.
  • Total 35 questions · 45 marks · Time: 60 minutes. There is no negative marking.
  • Topics: numbers & number names, + − × ÷, fractions, length, weight, capacity, temperature, time, money, geometry and data handling.

2. How to answer

  • Click one option (A, B, C or D). The next question opens automatically.
  • Use Previous / Next or the question numbers on the right to move around. Green = answered, grey = not answered.
  • You can change an answer any time before the exam ends.
  • The Submit button works only after all 35 questions are answered.
  • The timer cannot be paused. When time is over, the exam is submitted automatically.
  • Do not refresh, close or switch the page. Keep paper and pencil ready for rough work.

3. Camera rules

  • Your camera must stay on. Sit on your seat and always face the camera. Looking down at your paper is fine.
  • If you look left or right, or leave the seat, you will see a warning on the right side at once, and it becomes stronger if you keep looking away:
    Yellow – immediately Orange – 10 sec Red (severe) – 30 sec
  • The exam does not stop because of a warning, but all warnings are written in your report.
  • No one else should help or sit near you. No books, phones or calculators.

4. After the exam

  • You will get your score, section-wise marks, a full answer report and a certificate that you can download as PDF.
